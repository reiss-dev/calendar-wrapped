Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

The short version

Your calendar is read and analysed in your browser. It is never uploaded to us, and we never see it.

Calendar files

When you choose an .ics file, your browser reads it on your device. Every number, chart and image is worked out there. Nothing from the file is sent to our servers. A summary of the numbers is kept in your browser’s local storage so the page survives a refresh. “Start over” deletes it.

Google Calendar

If you choose Connect Google Calendar, Google asks you to grant read-only access to your calendar events. Your browser then fetches your events directly from Google and analyses them on your device, exactly as with a file. The access token stays in memory and is revoked as soon as your events have loaded. We never receive the token or your events.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is used only to show you your own summary, is not stored by us, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train models.

Shared links and images

A shared link contains only the numbers shown on your card, packed into the part of the address after the #, which browsers do not send to servers. Names and meeting titles are left out unless you switch them on. Images you save are created on your device.

Team reports

Team reports are built in your browser from files you add. Please ask your colleagues before using their calendars. Nothing is uploaded.

If you ask us to email your plan

This is the only time anything derived from your calendar reaches us. We send the plan text to the address you give, using our email provider, and we do not keep the plan. If you tick the January check-in box, we keep your email address and the date until we send that one email, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, not your card details.

Analytics

We count a handful of anonymous events, such as “story finished” or “card saved”, as daily totals. There are no cookies, no identifiers, no IP addresses and no calendar data in these counts.
