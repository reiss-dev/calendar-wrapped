Help us test with your calendar

Calendars vary a lot, especially from Outlook. This page checks how well yours is understood. It runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded unless you choose to send us something.

You get two things to send back if you’re happy to: a short report with counts only, and an anonymised copy of your file where every title, name, email and place is replaced, keeping only times and structure.


      

        
        
      

      
Have a look at the anonymised file before sending it. Open it in any text editor.
