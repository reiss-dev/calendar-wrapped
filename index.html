  1. Open Google Calendar settings, then Import & export.
  2. Click Export. A zip file downloads.
  3. Unzip it and choose the file named after your email address, ending in .ics.

On a phone, switch to the desktop site. Google only shows Export there.

  1. In Outlook on the web, open Settings, then Calendar, then Shared calendars.
  2. Under Publish a calendar, pick your calendar with Can view all details, and click Publish.
  3. Copy the ICS link, open it in a new tab to download the file, then choose it here. Unpublish afterwards if you like.

In desktop Outlook, use File, then Save Calendar, choosing the whole calendar with full details.

  1. Open Calendar on your Mac and select the calendar in the sidebar.
  2. Choose File, then Export, then Export… and save the file.
  3. Choose it here. iPhone can’t export directly, so use a Mac, or the Google or Outlook account behind it.
